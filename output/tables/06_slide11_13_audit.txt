'$0 incremental cost' (Slide 12, Recommendation 1) — CLASSIFICATION
==========================================================================================
This is NOT a statistically derived figure. It is a logical/definitional claim: the response_rate field
already exists in engagement.csv for every employee, so DETECTING the at-risk population requires no new
data collection or survey instrumentation. It does NOT mean the intervention (manager check-ins) has zero
labour cost -- that cost is real but unestimated in this analysis and should be caveated if pressed on it.
